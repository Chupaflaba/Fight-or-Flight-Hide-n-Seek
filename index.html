Install it

The installer puts every addon straight into your Garry's Mod folder. It takes about a minute.

Download installer

  1. Download the installerPress Download installer on this page. Open FightOrFlight_Installer.exe from your Downloads folder. It works from anywhere.
  2. Press Install / UpdateIt finds your Garry's Mod folder on its own. If it can't, press Change and pick the garrysmod\addons folder.
  3. Launch the gameWhen the bar finishes, press Launch Garry's Mod. To update later, open the installer again and press Install / Update.

Windows may warn that the installer is from an unknown publisher. Press More info, then Run anyway.
