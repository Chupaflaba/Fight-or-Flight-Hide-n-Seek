A warped cartoon Killer who uses ink and unnatural reach to control the battlefield. Inkway places ink puddles on walls that Redrawn Bend-O-Tron can travel between, allowing him to quickly reposition across the map. Ink Snare deploys a cardboard cutout surrounded by ink that reaches out and grabs nearby Hiders, slowing them down. With Rubber Reach, he stretches his hand an incredible distance to grab unsuspecting Hiders from afar.

A deceptive Killer built around repositioning, area control, and unpredictable long-range attacks.
