When the hunger takes over, run.

  • Health875 / Hider

A savage Killer that overwhelms Hiders through explosive attacks and bursts of uncontrollable fury. Gobble Bombs launches three bombs that detonate after a short timer, damaging nearby Hiders, or instantly explode when they make contact with a player. Frenzy sends Wyst into a violent state, granting increased movement speed and the ability to see Hiders through walls for 15 seconds, but comes with an extremely long cooldown. With Rampage, Wyst violently slams the ground three times, damaging all Hiders caught within the surrounding area with each impact.

A ferocious Killer built around explosive pressure, area damage, and devastating bursts of power.

Abilities

  • Gobble BombsR

    Wyst shoots out 3 Gobble Bombs that explode on a timer, dealing damage to nearby Hiders. The bombs instantly detonate when they come into contact with a player.

  • FrenzyT

    Wyst enters a frenzied state, gaining increased movement speed and the ability to see Hiders through walls for 15 seconds. This powerful ability comes with an extremely long cooldown.

  • RampageG

    Wyst violently slams the ground 3 times, dealing damage to Hiders within the surrounding area with each impact.
