Phantom Puppet

Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

Every gift comes with a price.

  • Health500 / Hider
  • Speed580

A deceptive spectral Killer that turns harmless presents into deadly traps. Night Vision grants Phantom Puppet enhanced vision for 12 seconds and can be toggled off to conserve its charge. Place Cursed Presents around the map for Hiders to discover and destroy, then launch a Puppet Curse to latch onto and slow a Hider, forcing them to rapidly press E or R to break free before it explodes. Hitting a Cursed Present with the Puppet Curse supercharges it, causing a devastating explosion that deals massive damage. With Full House, having five active presents grants Phantom Puppet extreme speed and the ability to see all Hiders.

A deceptive Killer built around traps, pressure, and turning seemingly harmless presents into deadly threats.

Abilities

  • Night VisionF

    Grants Phantom Puppet night vision for 12 seconds. Able to be toggled on and off to recharge.

  • Cursed PresentR

    Places a haunted present that players can destroy by holding the use button.

  • Puppet CurseT

    Launches a ghostly puppet that latches onto a Hider, slowing them. The victim can rapidly press E or R to break free before it explodes. If you hit a Cursed Present with this ability, the present will become super charged, exploding and dealing massive damage to any Hiders caught by it.

  • Full HousePassive

    Having 5 presents active grants Phantom Puppet the ability to see all Hiders, as well as extreme speed.
