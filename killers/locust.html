A surveillance-based Killer who turns the map into a network of eyes. Deploy TV allows Locust to place up to seven TVs that he can see through, highlighting any players caught within their view. With Televisional Pursuit, Locust can travel through any of his deployed TVs, allowing him to quickly reposition and hunt down detected Hiders. Channel Chaos deploys a larger TV that slows players caught within its radius, giving Locust greater control over key areas.

A strategic Killer built around surveillance, rapid repositioning, and map control.
