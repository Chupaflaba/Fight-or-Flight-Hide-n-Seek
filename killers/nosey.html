A relentless sensory Killer that uses sound and technology to hunt down Hiders. Sonar Pulse scans the surrounding area with echolocation, highlighting and slowing any Hiders caught in its range. Manhack Swarm summons a deadly drone that follows Nosey until it detects a player, then pursues and attacks them. When surrounded, Self-Destruct unleashes a devastating explosion after a brief charge-up, though Nosey must recover before returning to the hunt. Resonance allows Nosey to recharge his echolocation faster while standing still.

A tracking-focused Killer built around detection, pursuit, and decision making.
