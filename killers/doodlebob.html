Doodlebob

Executioner★★★★★Difficulty

Erase reality. Draw your own rules.

  • Health425 / Hider
  • Speed575

Armed with a pencil and an imagination that defies reality, DoodleBob can use Eraser Rift to tear open portals and travel through them before reappearing elsewhere. Sketch Sight reveals every Hider's location by outlining them with his pencil—but also reveals DoodleBob himself. His Doodleball launches a cartoon bowling ball that can ricochet up to three times, while Boom Sketch lets him draw explosive bombs that detonate when stepped on. Hit a Boom Sketch with a Doodleball to create a devastating Bowling Bomb.

A chaotic Killer built around tracking, unpredictable movement, ricochets, and creative traps.

Abilities

  • Eraser RiftT

    Erase a hole in reality, creating a portal that DoodleBob is able to travel through for a short duration before coming back up.

  • Sketch SightR

    Every hider is outlined by Doodlebob's pencil. Drawback is DoodleBob will outline himself as well.

  • DoodleballB

    Throws a cartoon bowling ball that ricochets up to 3 times.

  • Boom SketchG

    Draw an explosive bomb onto the ground that whenever hiders step on, will detonate. If a Doodleball is thrown upon this bomb, it will become an upgraded Bowling Bomb.
