Retro Springtrap

Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

Once you're caught, the hunt begins.

  • Health575 / Hider
  • Speed590

A relentless Killer armed with hatchets and deadly precision. Night Vision grants Retro Springtrap enhanced vision for 12 seconds and can be toggled off to conserve its charge. Hatchet Shot hurls an axe that embeds itself in a Hider, causing them to slowly bleed out while slowing them until the axe is removed. The victim or a teammate can hold E to remove it. Phantom Hatchet throws a spectral axe that travels forward before returning like a boomerang, damaging Hiders on both passes and passing straight through walls and objects.

A relentless Killer built around ranged pressure, persistent damage, and attacks that strike from unexpected angles.
