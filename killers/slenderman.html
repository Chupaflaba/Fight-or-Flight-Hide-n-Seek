Slenderman

Executioner★★★★★Difficulty

Don't let him see you looking.

  • Health500 / Hider
  • Speed500

A terrifying supernatural Killer who exists between the physical and spiritual worlds. Manifest allows Slenderman to enter the physical world, giving him the ability to directly interact with and hunt Hiders. Use Dark Grasp to extend a tentacle toward a Hider, pulling them toward Slenderman if it connects. Entanglement releases several tentacles around Slenderman, ensnaring nearby Hiders, slowing their movement and limiting how far they can escape. With The Gaze, Slenderman's physical form is limited by a red movement meter that depletes as he moves and can only be restored when Hiders look at him. However, any Hider who looks at Slenderman when he first manifests becomes fixated on him, locking their camera onto him and blurring their vision.

A supernatural Killer built around fear, forced attention, cut offs, and smart positioning.

Abilities

  • Manifest1

    Slenderman enters the physical world, allowing him to interact with and hunt Hiders directly.

  • Dark Grasp2

    Slenderman extends a tentacle toward a Hider. If it connects, the Hider will be pulled towards Slenderman for a short duration.

  • EntanglementG

    Slenderman releases several tentacles around himself. Hiders caught within them become ensnared, slowing their movement and restricting how far they can escape from Slenderman.

  • The GazePassive

    Slenderman has limited movement in the physical world, represented by a red meter. Once depleted, he cannot move in his physical form. Looking at Slenderman in his physical form restores a portion of this meter. Hiders who look at Slenderman when he first manifests become fixated on him, locking their camera on him and blurring their vision.
