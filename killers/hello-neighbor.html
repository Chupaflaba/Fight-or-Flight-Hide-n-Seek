H

Hello Neighbor

Executioner★★★★★Difficulty

You never know what's waiting around the corner.

  • Health500 / Hider
  • Speed565

Hunt down Hiders with the help of Night Vision, granting Hello Neighbor enhanced vision for 12 seconds and allowing him to toggle it off to conserve its charge. Place Bear Traps to catch unsuspecting Hiders, or deploy Security Cameras to monitor key areas and alert you when Hiders pass by. When they try to make a getaway, use Glue Traps to create sticky puddles that slow Hiders caught within them.

A methodical Killer built around surveillance, traps, and controlling the map.

Abilities

  • Night VisionF

    Grants Hello Neighbor night vision for 12 seconds. Able to be toggled on and off to recharge.

  • Bear TrapR

    Places a bear trap that catches Hiders. Can be broken.

  • Security CameraT

    Attaches a camera to a surface. This camera highlights Hiders that go past it and alert Hello Neighbor to their position. Can be broken.

  • Glue TrapG

    Launches a glue trap that explodes into a puddle, slowing Hiders within it.
