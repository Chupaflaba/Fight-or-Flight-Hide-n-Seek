Rolling Giant

Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

Once it starts rolling, get out of the way.

  • Health500 / Hider
  • Speed560

A terrifying Killer built around momentum and overwhelming force. Roll Rush starts slowly before rapidly accelerating, turning Rolling Giant into an unstoppable threat that deals absurd damage to any Hider caught in its path. Crashing into a wall cancels the rush, giving Hiders a narrow chance to escape. Use Predator Sense to detect and highlight nearby Hiders within a short radius. When closing the distance, Rolling Dash launches Rolling Giant forward twice in rapid intervals, damaging any Hiders caught in either dash.

A relentless Killer built around momentum, pursuit, and devastating high-speed attacks.

Abilities

  • Roll RushT

    Starts slowly before rapidly accelerating. Any Hider caught in Rolling Giant's path takes absurd damage. If crashing into a wall while Roll Rushing, the ability is cancelled.

  • Predator SenseR

    Detects nearby Hiders in a small radius and highlights those it senses.

  • Rolling DashG

    Rolling Giant dashes out twice in intervals, Hiders hit by these dashes take damage.
