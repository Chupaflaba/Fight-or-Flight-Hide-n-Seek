A relentless Killer who becomes increasingly dangerous the longer she hunts. Mind Grip fuels Agatha's growing Bloodlust, increasing her movement speed and attack speed as she chases Hiders. With Tag You're It, Agatha charges up before violently sprinting in the direction she's aiming, instantly downing any Hider caught in her path. If she fails to catch anyone, she'll throw a tantrum. Use Peekaboo to vanish before the eyes of Hiders, creating a brief opportunity to reposition before suddenly reappearing. Her Bloodlust can reach up to three levels, with each level making her faster and more aggressive.

A relentless pursuit Killer built around escalating aggression, instant downs, and deceptive repositioning.
