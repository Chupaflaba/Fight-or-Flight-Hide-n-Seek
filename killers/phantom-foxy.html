Phantom Foxy

Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

You won't see the end coming.

  • Health425 / Hider
  • Speed600

Abilities

  • Night VisionF

    Grants Phantom Foxy night vision for 12 seconds. Able to be toggled on and off to recharge.

  • Phantom PounceR

    Leaps a long distance and instantly downs any player caught.

  • Spectral DashT

    Dashes Foxy forward in the direction they're moving.

A relentless spectral Killer built for sudden, devastating attacks. Night Vision grants Phantom Foxy enhanced vision for 12 seconds and can be toggled off to conserve its charge. Use Phantom Pounce to leap a tremendous distance and instantly down any Hider caught in the attack. When closing the distance, Spectral Dash propels Foxy forward in the direction he's moving, allowing him to rapidly chase down fleeing Hiders.

A deadly pressure Killer built around explosive mobility, one shot ability, and relentless pursuit.
