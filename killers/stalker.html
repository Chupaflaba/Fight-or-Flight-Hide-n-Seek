The Stalker

Executioner★★★★★Difficulty

The more he watches, the more dangerous he becomes.

  • Health500 / Hider
  • Speed460

A calculating Killer who grows stronger through observation, using fear and relentless tracking to hunt down Hiders. Obsession allows The Stalker to grab a Hider and mark them as his new obsession after holding them for 4 seconds. His obsession remains permanently highlighted through walls, allowing him to track their movements until he chooses another target. Use Ringtone to call the nearest Hider, regardless of distance, forcing them to emit a loud audio cue that reveals their location while significantly slowing them until the ringtone ends. Evil Eye launches a projectile that explodes on contact, damaging and briefly blinding Hiders caught in its blast. Every successful hit also grants additional Stalk. With Stalk, The Stalker can crouch and observe Hiders to build his meter, increasing his movement speed and Evil Eye damage with each level, up to a maximum of five. While actively stalking, the round timer progresses at just one-quarter of its normal speed.

A methodical tracking Killer built around observation, escalating power, target fixation, and relentless pursuit.

Abilities

  • ObsessionRMB

    The Stalker grabs a Hider for 4 seconds, marking them as his obsession. His obsession is permanently highlighted through walls, but can be replaced by grabbing another Hider.

  • RingtoneG

    The Stalker calls the nearest Hider, regardless of distance. The targeted Hider emits a ringtone, revealing their location through audio while being significantly slowed until it ends.

  • Evil EyeR

    The Stalker launches an explosive projectile that damages and briefly blinds Hiders on contact. Each successful hit grants additional Stalk.

  • StalkPassive

    While crouching, the Stalker observes Hiders to build his Stalk meter. Each level increases his movement speed and Evil Eye damage, up to level 5. While stalking, the round timer moves at 1/4 of its normal speed.
