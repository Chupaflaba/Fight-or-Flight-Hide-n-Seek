Huntsman Spider

Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

You are its prey.

  • Health500 / Hider
  • Speed600

A relentless predator built for hunting from the shadows. Night Vision grants the Huntsman Spider enhanced vision for 20 seconds and can be toggled off to conserve its charge. Lay down Web Snares to trap unsuspecting Hiders, or slip into Camo, vanishing from Hiders until they are almost on top of it and downing anyone it bites from behind. Its Predatory DNA enhances its night vision duration and greatly increases its climbing speed.

An insidious Killer built around ambushes, traps, and superior mobility.

Abilities

  • Night VisionF

    Grants Huntsman Spider night vision for 20 seconds. Able to be toggled on and off to recharge.

  • Web SnareE

    Lays down a web that traps players who walk into it.

  • CamoR

    Huntsman Spider turns translucent and can only be seen by Hiders from a short distance away, moving at half speed while it lasts. Biting a Hider from behind in this form downs them instantly. Press R again to drop the camo.

  • Predatory DNAPassive

    Huntsman Spider's night vision's duration is longer, and their climbing speed is greatly enhanced.
