Obelisk

Executioner★★★★★Difficulty

Darkness is the only thing you can trust.

  • Health500 / Hider
  • Speed1060

A supernatural Killer who controls the battlefield through darkness and sudden bursts of movement. Night Vision grants Obelisk enhanced vision for 12 seconds and can be toggled off to conserve its charge. Use Shadow Wall to raise a silent barrier that cuts off paths and traps Hiders, or place a Shadow Anchor to instantly warp back to its location. Cursed Eyes temporarily blinds every Hider across the map, creating openings to strike, while Surge lets Obelisk burst forward with a sudden increase of speed.

A versatile Killer built around timing, positioning, and controlling the Hiders' escape routes.

Abilities

  • Night VisionF

    Grants Obelisk night vision for 12 seconds. Able to be toggled on and off to recharge.

  • Shadow WallG

    Raises a silent barrier of darkness, cutting off paths and trapping Hiders. Disappears after a while.

  • Shadow AnchorT

    Leaves behind a shadow clone that Obelisk can instantly warp back to.

  • Cursed EyesR

    Curses the eyes of every Hider, blinding them for a short duration.

  • SurgeSHIFT

    Bursts forward with a sudden surge of speed.
