Plushtrap

Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

Small, silent, and always one pounce away.

  • Health300 / Hider
  • Speed300

Abilities

  • Night VisionF

    Grants Plushtrap night vision for 12 seconds. Can be toggled on and off to recharge.

  • Plush PounceLMB

    Plushtrap pounces in the direction he's aiming, bouncing off walls to reach Hiders. The higher he is when pouncing, the more damage he deals upon landing. A successful pounce activates Hide then Seek.

  • Back ShredderR

    Plushtrap dashes forward, attaching himself to the back of any Hider hit. He attacks three times before leaping off in the direction they're facing.

  • Hide then SeekPassive

    After a successful Plush Pounce, Plushtrap enters stealth for 1 second, during which he cannot attack but can move undetected. Once ready, he can press LMB to redeploy his pounce.

A cunning ambush Killer that uses explosive leaps and stealth to catch Hiders off guard. Plush Pounce launches Plushtrap toward his target, allowing him to bounce off walls to reach unexpected angles. The higher he is when initiating the pounce, the more damage he deals upon landing. Successfully landing a pounce activates Hide then Seek, allowing Plushtrap to move undetected for 1 second while unable to attack before preparing another pounce. With Back Shredder, Plushtrap dashes forward and latches onto the back of any Hider he hits, attacking them three times before leaping off in the direction they're facing.

A stealthy ambush Killer built around aerial attacks, hit-and-run tactics, and chaining pounces into surprise attacks.
