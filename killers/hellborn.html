Hellborn

Let the world burn.

  • Health575 / Hider

Abilities

  • BlazethrowerRMB

    Hellborn unleashes a continuous stream of flames from his mouth while moving at reduced speed. The attack has limited fuel but can be cancelled early by releasing RMB.

  • Spark ShotsR

    Hellborn launches three fiery projectiles that spread apart before arcing downward and exploding on impact. Holding R instead fires a larger, faster fireball that travels straight without arcing.

  • SupernovaG

    Hellborn engulfs himself in intense flames, gaining a burst of speed before unleashing a fiery explosion that damages nearby Hiders.

  • Infectious FlamesPassive

    Hellborn's fire-based abilities inflict Infectious Flames, causing Hiders to burn and take damage every second until they extinguish the flames.

A fiery Killer that overwhelms Hiders with relentless flames and explosive attacks. Blazethrower unleashes a continuous stream of fire from Hellborn's mouth while reducing his movement speed. The flames can only be sustained for a limited time before his fuel runs out, but the attack can be cancelled early by releasing RMB. Use Spark Shots to launch three fiery projectiles that spread apart before arcing downward, exploding into flames upon hitting a surface or Hider and damaging anything caught in the blast. Holding R instead fires a single larger, faster fireball that does not arc downward. With Supernova, Hellborn engulfs his entire body in intense flames, gaining a burst of movement speed before unleashing a devastating explosion around himself. His Infectious Flames passive causes all fire-based abilities to ignite Hiders, dealing damage every second until they manage to extinguish the flames.

A devastating area-denial Killer built around persistent burning damage, explosive attacks, and overwhelming firepower.
