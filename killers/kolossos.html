Kolossos

Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

Raw velocity. Relentless pursuit.

  • Health425 / Hider
  • Speed600

A monstrous close-range Killer built around overwhelming force and relentless aggression. Rampage delivers rapid attacks or can be charged into a powerful forward dash, dealing increased damage. Gore Rush allows Kolossos to impale and carry Hiders, dealing bonus damage when they're slammed into a wall. Rend grabs a Hider and tears into them before violently throwing them away. Bloodsense reveals weakened Hiders at 25 HP or below, allowing Kolossos to hunt down the injured. With Wallbound, Kolossos can leap off walls to pursue his prey from unexpected angles.

A devastating Killer built around explosive speed, relentless pursuit, and finishing weakened prey.

Abilities

  • RampageLMB

    Pressing LMB fast will do a normal attack, slightly nudging you forward. If LMB is held, Kolossos will dash forward dealing increased damage.

  • Gore RushR

    Charges forward, impaling players and carrying them along. Crashing them into a wall deals bonus damage.

  • RendF

    Grabs a player and viciously slashes them before throwing them away.

  • BloodsenseRMB

    Detects weakened players who're at 25 HP or below.

  • WallboundPassive

    Allows Kolossos to jump off walls.
