The Omnidroid

Executioner★★★★★Difficulty

Built to destroy. Programmed to hunt.

  • Health875 / Hider
  • Speed570

Abilities

  • Target LockG

    Omnidroid grabs a hider, allowing him to throw them anywhere he pleases. If thrown at another hider, it will deal damage to them on impact.

  • Impact SlamSPACE

    Omnidroid leaps high into the air before crashing down, dealing damage to all Hiders caught within the impact zone. While leaping, Omnidroid gains Super Armor, making him impervious to damage and various forms of stun.

  • Rolling AssaultR

    Omnidroid transforms into a rolling ball, rapidly gaining speed. Players struck by him take damage and are launched a considerable distance.

A relentless combat machine designed to overwhelm Hiders through brute force and devastating mobility. Target Lock grabs a Hider and allows Omnidroid to throw them in any direction, dealing additional damage if the victim collides with another Hider. Use Impact Slam to leap high into the air before crashing down, damaging all Hiders caught in the impact zone. While airborne, Omnidroid gains Super Armor, making him immune to damage and various forms of stun. With Rolling Assault, Omnidroid transforms into a rolling ball and rapidly accelerates, damaging and launching any Hider caught in his path.

A devastating Killer built around brute force, crowd control, mobility, and overwhelming attacks.
