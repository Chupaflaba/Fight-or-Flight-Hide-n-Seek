Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

You can't outrun a ghost.

  • Health425 / Hider
  • Speed500

Abilities

  • Night VisionF

    Grants Phantom Bonnie night vision for 12 seconds. Able to be toggled on and off to recharge.

  • Phantom RushSHIFT

    Dashes in a straight direction at a fast speed, attacking any player in the way.

  • Death DecoyT

    Allows Phantom Bonnie to hover, fly, and pass through physical objects.

A spectral Killer that moves freely through the physical world. Night Vision grants Phantom Bonnie enhanced vision for 12 seconds and can be toggled off to conserve its charge. Use Phantom Rush to dash forward at incredible speed, attacking any Hider caught in the way. Death Decoy sends a deceptive decoy in the direction you're facing, confusing Hiders and creating opportunities to strike. With Spectral Form, Phantom Bonnie can hover, fly, and pass straight through physical objects.

A supernatural Killer built around deception, confusing pursuit, and unrestricted movement.
