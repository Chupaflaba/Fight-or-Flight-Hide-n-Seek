Phantom Freddy

Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

You heard him. Nowhere to run.

  • Health500 / Hider
  • Speed600

Abilities

  • Night VisionF

    Grants Phantom Freddy night vision for 12 seconds. Able to be toggled on and off to recharge.

  • Dreadful EchoR

    Releases a deafening screech that echolocates nearby Hiders, highlighting those detected and briefly disorienting them.

  • Echo ShiftT

    Teleports Phantom Freddy behind the nearest highlighted Hider.

A fast-paced scouting Killer that hunts through sound and sudden repositioning. Night Vision grants Phantom Freddy enhanced vision for 12 seconds and can be toggled off to conserve its charge. Dreadful Echo unleashes a deafening screech that echolocates nearby Hiders, highlighting those detected and briefly disorienting them. Once a target is revealed, Echo Shift allows Phantom Freddy to teleport directly behind the nearest highlighted Hider, turning detection into an immediate ambush.

A tracking-focused Killer built around echolocation, disorientation, and surprise attacks.
