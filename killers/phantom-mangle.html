Phantom Mangle

Pursuer★★★★★Difficulty

Static in the dark. Death in the shadows.

  • Health425 / Hider
  • Speed600

A terrifying disruptive Killer that overwhelms Hiders with disorientation and relentless speed. Night Vision grants Phantom Mangle enhanced vision for 12 seconds and can be toggled off to conserve its charge. Distorted Screech unleashes a deafening burst that greatly impairs the vision and hearing of nearby Hiders while highlighting them. Charge up Electric Slam to launch Phantom Mangle into the air and crash down, damaging Hiders caught in the impact and leaving behind a field of electricity that continues to harm anyone who crosses it. With Prowl, crouching builds a meter that is automatically consumed while running, granting Phantom Mangle enhanced movement speed.

A relentless Killer built around sensory disruption, area denial, and explosive bursts of speed.

Abilities

  • Night VisionF

    Grants Phantom Mangle night vision for 12 seconds. Able to be toggled on and off to recharge.

  • Distorted ScreechR

    Releases a loud screech that disorients nearby players, impairing their vision greatly and hearing while highlighting them.

  • Electric SlamG

    Phantom Mangle charges up the electricity from her circuits, before leaping into the air and landing on the ground. Anyone hit within her landing radius will take immediate damage. This ability also leaves electricity for a short period of time within her landing radius, damaging Hiders that walk over it.

  • ProwlPassive

    When crouching, Phantom Mangle builds her prowl bar (seen on bottom left). Exhaust this bar automatically when running in exchange for enhanced movement speed.
