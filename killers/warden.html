Warden

Executioner★★★★★Difficulty

You can't hide from what can hear you.

  • Health500 / Hider
  • Speed555

A terrifying subterranean Killer that hunts Hiders through sound, scent, and fear. Burrow allows Warden to dig underground and travel beneath the map unseen, emerging wherever he chooses. Use Sonic Boom to charge and unleash a powerful long-range sonic blast aimed toward your crosshair. Predator's Scent detects nearby Hiders and highlights those within its range, while Dread Aura darkens the vision of Hiders who venture too close. With Keen Hearing, Warden can hear walking and sprinting Hiders from a great distance and highlight them within his detection range—but crouching or crouch-walking allows Hiders to remain undetected.

A relentless tracking Killer built around stealth, sensory detection, and controlling the map.

Abilities

  • BurrowR

    Warden digs underground, allowing him to travel beneath the map unseen. Press R again to emerge at his current location.

  • Sonic BoomSPACE

    Warden charges and unleashes a powerful sonic blast from a distance, aimed toward the player's crosshair.

  • Predator's ScentT

    Warden detects the scent of nearby Hiders, highlighting any players within his detection radius.

  • Dread AuraPassive

    Warden radiates an aura of fear that darkens the vision of nearby Hiders.

  • Keen HearingPassive

    Warden can hear Hiders from a great distance while they are walking or sprinting, highlighting them within his detection range. Hiders can avoid detection by crouching or crouch-walking.
