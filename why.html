Why an installer?

Most Garry's Mod content comes from the Steam Workshop. This gamemode doesn't, and here's why.

  • Some addons can't go on the WorkshopThe gamemode includes modified versions of other people's addons and content. The Workshop only accepts content you made or have permission to upload, and reuploads risk being taken down. An installer lets all of it still be shared in one place.
  • One click, everythingNo subscribing to dozens of separate addons and hoping you got them all.
  • Everyone on the same versionEvery player gets the same versions of every addon, so nothing breaks because of an older or different copy.
  • Small, fast updatesAfter the first install it only downloads the addons that changed, so most updates take seconds.
  • Updates the moment they're outNew killers and fixes reach you as soon as they're released, with no waiting on a review.
  • Safe and simpleIt only downloads from this project's GitHub release and only writes into your garrysmod\addons folder. The source is on GitHub if you want to check.
